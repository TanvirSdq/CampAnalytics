Methodology & Usage Guide

Understand how the suite turns Wikimedia Commons campaign data into retention and health signals.

Quick guide

1 · Retention Analytics

Enter two or more campaign codes, such as wlmbd22 wlmbd23. Run the analysis, then choose a table, heatmap, or world map to compare contributor overlap across campaigns.

2 · Health Evaluation

Enter one target code, select a previous-year or custom baseline, choose a region, and evaluate. Review the five weighted indicators and the peer-relative diagnostic insights.

Use this tab as the reference layer: it explains the data sources, processing stages, scoring model, and interpretation limits behind the two analysis modes.
